What We Do

Our Services

From a single sofa to a full estate cleanout, TEC handles every job with care, speed, and a commitment to eco-friendly disposal.

01 🛋️
Furniture Removal

Sofas, beds, dressers, tables — we remove all types of furniture from any room in your home or office, fast and careful.

02 🧺
Appliance Hauling

Refrigerators, washers, dryers, dishwashers — heavy appliance removal done safely, with eco-friendly disposal options.

03 🏚️
Estate Cleanouts

Respectful, thorough cleanout services for estates, foreclosures, and hoarder homes. We handle the heavy lifting so you don't have to.

04 🪵
Construction Debris

Drywall, lumber, roofing materials, concrete — we haul post-renovation or post-construction debris from job sites of all sizes.

05 🌿
Yard Waste Removal

Branches, brush, leaves, old mulch, and more. Keep your yard clean and tidy — we'll haul away all the organic debris.

06 🚗
Garage & Basement Cleanouts

Reclaim your space. We'll clear out years of clutter from garages, basements, attics, and storage units efficiently.
